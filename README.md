## About Groupe Ecs

**Groupe Ecs** is a **Ghost Kitchen** in **Boston** — Virtual brands optimized for delivery without dining room overhead.

We publish hours, offerings and contact paths clearly so customers know what to expect before they visit or book.

---

## Why we exist

Groupe Ecs exists to make ghost kitchen feel personal in Boston — clear pricing, honest recommendations, and a team that remembers why you came back.

- Rooted in Boston with regulars who know our name
- Offerings that match what we publish online
- Support you can reach during real hours
- A space built for repeat visits, not one-offs

---

## Where we're headed

We're growing Groupe Ecs into a food service people recommend without hesitation — more depth in our craft, same straightforward experience at the door.
